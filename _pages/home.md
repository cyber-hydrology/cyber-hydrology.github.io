<h1 style = "margin-bottom:20px;"> Welcome to Noh Lab </h1>

[금오공과대학교](https://www.kumoh.ac.kr) [토목공학과](https://civil.kumoh.ac.kr) **수문학연구실 (Noh Lab)**에서는 기후변화로 인해 인류에게 직접적인 영향을 미치는 홍수, 가뭄 등 물 문제에 대한 분석 및 해결 방법에 대해 연구합니다.

We are a **Hydrology and Water Resources Lab (Noh Lab)** at Civil Engineering Department at [Kumoh National Institute of Technology](https://eng.kumoh.ac.kr). Our aim is to explore and characterize complexity and uncertainty in hydrology and water resources systems to improve both our understanding of these systems and our ability to respond to water-related hazards under changing climate conditions, leveraging physics-based modeling, AI/deep learning, high-performance computing, and digital twins.

주요 연구분야:

There are five main areas of research:

1. **도시 홍수 해석 (Urban flood modeling)** : Hyper-resolution 1D–2D urban flood modeling accelerated by multi-GPU high-performance computing, and digital twins for real-time flood emergency response in resilient and sustainable cities
2. **수문 자료 동화 (Hydrologic data assimilation)** : Reducing predictive uncertainty by combining model simulations with observations, including multivariate assimilation of sewer, surface, and camera data for real-time flood forecasting
3. **AI·딥러닝 수문 해석 (AI & deep learning for hydrology)** : Physics-guided deep learning, super-resolution downscaling of precipitation and flood maps, and fast surrogate models to solve complex water-related environmental problems
4. **기후변화 적응 수자원 관리 (Climate-adaptive water resources management)** : Storyline and large-ensemble assessment of tropical cyclones, extreme precipitation, and compound flood–drought risks, with adaptive and resilient water management strategies
5. **멀티센서 수문 관측 (Measurement and analysis of hydrologic variables using multi-sensors)** : Utilization of multiple sensors to measure hydrologic variables from precipitation to soil moisture to streamflow, and observation-constrained high-resolution flood risk mapping

주요 논문은 [Publications](publications) 페이지에서 확인하실 수 있습니다. See our [Publications](publications) for selected recent work.

<h3 style = "font-size:30px; margin-top:40px;"> 국제공동연구 International Collaboration </h3>
Noh Lab collaborates with leading universities, research institutes, and industry partners around the world, such as NCAR, Kyoto University, Tohoku University, University of Michigan, Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, and NVIDIA, to advance flood and water-hazard research.

노성진 연구실은 NCAR, Kyoto University, Tohoku University, University of Michigan, Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, NVIDIA 등 세계 주요 대학·연구기관·기업과 국제공동연구를 수행합니다.
