**HERA 2026(Hydro-Environment Resilience Annual) Symposium** was held on March 6–7, 2026, at the Kyowon Dogo Training Center. 
It brought together researchers to share ideas and recent progress, with presentation sessions on flood modeling, geohazard analysis, hydrologic prediction, and climate change modeling.
